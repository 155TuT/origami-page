models/crane/creases.svg is the traditional crane crease pattern flat_crane.svg
from Origami Simulator by Amanda Ghassaei. The topology in models/crane/poses.fold
and the packed runtime asset crane-data.js were derived from this pattern.
https://github.com/amandaghassaei/OrigamiSimulator/blob/main/assets/Origami/flat_crane.svg
The sampled animation, page design, and browser renderer were created for this demo.
